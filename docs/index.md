# Macro Dials

Long-form articles, the models behind them, and the data feed that both are built from — a three-cycle read of where the debt, capital-spending and business cycles stand, written to be checkable.

## What is here

- **[Articles](blog/index.md)** — the long-form pieces. The current one is *Better Together Than Alone*, a snapshot as of the Nov 2, 2026 close on the SpaceX–Tesla thesis: the physics, the money and the cycle.
- **[Models](model/index.md)** — the spreadsheets. *SpaceX Bull Case 2.0* rebuilds the bull's bottoms-up model with the corrections the article identified; download the workbook or read its summary tables here, and start with [Three sets of numbers](model/three-numbers.md) to know what to expect from the company's figures, the bull's and the model's.
- **[The data feed](data.md)** — what the repository pulls twice a day and how the dashboard reads it.

## How to read any of it

Every probability on this site is either the dashboard's computed read or the market's own price. None is the author's conviction, and nothing here is advice. Where a number comes from a bull's spreadsheet or from management's ambition rather than from a filing, the text says so.

Three running numbers carry most of the story: gigawatts of compute energized, revenue per gigawatt against the cost to build one, and the mix of money funding the build. When the first rises on schedule and the second stays well above one, the strategy is working; when the third shifts from cash flow and vendors toward new equity and long-term contracts, the bridge is under strain.
